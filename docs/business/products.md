**For / mention when:**
- **Breweries & taprooms** — recurring game nights, tournaments (Bru Bag and Giant Beer Pong were built for this), patio-sized picks. Mention for any brewery, taproom, beer garden, or cidery.
- **Weddings & venues** — lawn-game hours between ceremony and reception; games that photograph well (Giant Jenga, Connect 4). Mention for wedding venues, barns, resorts, event spaces.
- **Corporate & HR** — company picnics, field days, team-building, office celebrations; we handle logistics so the organizer doesn't. Mention when the contact runs people ops, HR, office management, or internal events.
- **Community & everything else** — block parties, festivals, camps, fundraisers, birthday parties, bachelor/bachelorette.
